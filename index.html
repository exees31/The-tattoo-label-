Frequently Asked
Ink Q&A

Q: How long does a tattoo take to sink in and heal?

A: Surface healing typically takes 2-4 weeks. However, it takes about 3 to 6 months for the deeper layers of skin to completely regenerate and for the ink to fully "settle" or sink into the dermis.

Q: Will my tattoo fade over time?

A: Yes, all tattoos age naturally. The best way to keep your ink looking vibrant and prevent premature fading is strict sun protection and daily moisturizing.

Q: Can I get a tattoo over a scar?

A: Often, yes! It depends on the age and severity of the scar. We recommend a consultation so our artists can evaluate the tissue and design something that flows perfectly.
